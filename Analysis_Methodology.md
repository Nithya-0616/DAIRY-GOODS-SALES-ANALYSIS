# Analysis Methodology – Dairy Goods Sales Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyses dairy goods sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand revenue performance, product contribution, brand performance, regional distribution and sales trends.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts and an interactive dashboard.

## 2. Dataset Overview

The dataset contains **4,325 records and 23 columns**, covering dairy sales and farm-related information.

**Key data attributes:**

| Category | Attributes |
|---|---|
| Farm Information | Location, Total Land Area, Number of Cows, Farm Size |
| Product Information | Product ID, Product Name, Brand |
| Sales Information | Quantity Sold, Price per Unit, Approx. Total Revenue |
| Inventory Information | Quantity in Stock, Minimum Stock Threshold, Reorder Quantity |
| Product Storage | Shelf Life, Storage Condition |
| Date Information | Date, Production Date, Expiration Date |
| Business Information | Customer Location, Sales Channel |

**Analysis period:** 2019–2022

## 3. Data Preparation

The dataset was reviewed before performing the analysis.

The preparation process included:

- Reviewing the dataset structure and available columns.
- Checking the completeness of the data.
- Understanding the data types and business meaning of the fields.
- Identifying relevant measures and dimensions for sales analysis.
- Using the existing revenue and quantity fields for business performance calculations.

The source dataset contains no blank cells in the inspected records.

## 4. Analysis Methodology

Microsoft Excel was used to perform the analysis.

### 4.1 Pivot Tables

Pivot Tables were used to summarise sales and revenue across different business dimensions.

The analysis covered:

- Annual revenue performance.
- Revenue by geographical location.
- Product-wise revenue contribution.
- Brand-wise revenue contribution.
- Sales channel performance.
- Farm-size performance.

### 4.2 Pivot Charts

Pivot Charts were used to represent the summarised information visually.

The following chart types were incorporated:

- Doughnut and pie charts for contribution analysis.
- Line chart for annual sales trends.
- Bar charts for channel and farm-size comparisons.

### 4.3 Interactive Dashboard

An interactive dashboard was developed to bring the major KPIs and visualisations together.

Slicers were incorporated to allow users to filter the analysis by:

- Product Name
- Year

This allows users to explore sales performance across different products and years.

## 5. Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard highlights four important business KPIs.

| KPI | Result | Description |
|---|---:|---|
| Total Revenue | ₹58.73 Million | Approximate total revenue generated |
| Quantity Sold | 1,073,011 | Total quantity sold, measured in litres/kg |
| Average Shelf Life | 29.13 Days | Average product shelf life |
| Best-Selling Product | Curd | Product highlighted as the best seller |

### KPI Calculation Logic

- **Total Revenue:** Sum of Approx. Total Revenue (INR).
- **Quantity Sold:** Sum of Quantity Sold (liters/kg).
- **Average Shelf Life:** Average of Shelf Life (days).
- **Best-Selling Product:** Identified through product-level sales performance in the dashboard.

## 6. Dashboard Components

The dashboard contains six major analytical visualisations.

### 6.1 Product Sales Performance

Compares revenue contribution across dairy product categories such as Curd, Butter, Milk, Lassi, Paneer and other products.

**Purpose:** Identify products contributing significantly to overall revenue.

### 6.2 Brand-wise Sales

Displays revenue contribution across different dairy brands.

**Purpose:** Compare brand performance and identify leading contributors.

### 6.3 Regional Sales Performance

Compares revenue across geographical locations.

**Purpose:** Understand regional differences in sales performance.

### 6.4 Annual Sales Trend

A line chart representing annual revenue from 2019 to 2022.

**Purpose:** Understand year-over-year revenue movement and identify changes in business performance.

### 6.5 Sales by Channel

Compares sales performance across Wholesale, Retail and Online channels.

**Purpose:** Evaluate the contribution of different distribution channels.

### 6.6 Sales by Farm Size

Compares revenue generated by Large, Medium and Small farm categories.

**Purpose:** Understand how farm-size categories contribute to overall revenue.

## 7. Key Business Insights

Based on the Pivot Table analysis:

1. **Overall Revenue:** The dataset records approximately ₹58.73 million in total revenue.
2. **Product Performance:** Curd records the highest product-level revenue contribution, at approximately ₹6.74 million.
3. **Brand Performance:** Amul contributes the highest brand-wise revenue, at approximately ₹14.61 million.
4. **Regional Performance:** Chandigarh records the highest revenue among the locations in the regional analysis, at approximately ₹7.54 million.
5. **Annual Trend:** Revenue declines progressively between 2019 and 2022.
6. **Sales Channels:** Retail records the highest revenue among the three sales channels, followed by Wholesale and Online.
7. **Farm Size:** Large farms record the highest revenue contribution among the three farm-size categories.

## 8. Business Recommendations

Based on the observed performance:

- Investigate the reasons behind the declining annual revenue trend.
- Monitor demand for high-performing products such as Curd.
- Study the strategies and distribution strengths of leading brands.
- Explore opportunities to improve sales in lower-performing regions.
- Evaluate channel-specific performance to optimise distribution strategies.
- Review inventory levels and reorder thresholds to support better stock planning.
