# Dairy Goods Sales Analysis & Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project explores dairy product sales and business performance using Microsoft Excel. It focuses on analysing revenue trends, product performance, brand contribution, regional sales, distribution channels, and farm-size categories.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to convert the underlying dataset into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

- Analyse revenue performance across different years.
- Identify top-performing dairy products and brands.
- Compare sales performance across geographical regions.
- Understand revenue distribution across sales channels.
- Evaluate sales contribution by farm size.
- Present key business metrics through an interactive dashboard.

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains **4,325 records and 23 columns**, covering dairy production, inventory, and sales information.

Key attributes include:

- Location and farm size
- Number of cows and total land area
- Product name and brand
- Quantity and price per unit
- Total value and approximate revenue
- Shelf life and storage conditions
- Production and expiration dates
- Quantity sold and stock levels
- Customer location and sales channel
- Minimum stock threshold and reorder quantity

## Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard presents six major analytical views:

1. **Product Sales Performance** – Compares sales contribution across dairy product categories.
2. **Brand-wise Sales** – Evaluates revenue contribution from different dairy brands.
3. **Regional Sales Performance** – Compares sales across selected geographical regions.
4. **Annual Sales Trend** – Tracks revenue performance from 2019 to 2022.
5. **Sales by Channel** – Examines sales distribution across wholesale, retail, and online channels.
6. **Sales by Farm Size** – Compares sales performance across large, medium, and small farms.

## Key Business Insights

- Total approximate revenue across the dataset is ₹58.73 million.
- Curd is identified as the best-selling product in the dashboard.
- Amul records the highest brand-wise revenue contribution in the Pivot Table analysis.
- Annual revenue shows a declining trend between 2019 and 2022.
- Chandigarh records the highest regional revenue in the Pivot Table analysis.
- Sales performance varies across wholesale, retail, and online channels.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate the decline in annual revenue and identify the factors affecting sales.
- Review product-level demand to prioritise inventory and production planning.
- Strengthen strategies for high-performing brands while evaluating opportunities for other brands.
- Analyse regional sales differences to identify opportunities for market expansion.
- Review sales channel performance to improve distribution and sales strategies.
- Use stock thresholds and reorder quantities to support inventory planning.
